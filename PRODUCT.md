# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who want to inspect a complex real-life situation before acting: relationship decisions, career uncertainty, family pressure, social dynamics, and timing-sensitive choices. They arrive in a reflective, private state and need an interface that feels precise, protective, and unusually capable.

## Product Purpose

Astraloom turns fragments of lived reality into an evidence-linked simulation field. It helps users see people, pressure, uncertainty, timing, and next observable signals without claiming deterministic fate.

## Brand Personality

Forbidden observatory, quiet precision, luminous mystery.

## Anti-references

Avoid ordinary SaaS landing pages, left-copy/right-card hero layouts, decorative glass panels, purple-blue gradient AI polish, cheap neon gaming aesthetics, generic dashboard previews, editorial-tech tropes, and repeated tiny uppercase section labels.

## Design Principles

1. Make the first viewport feel like opening a rare instrument, not visiting a software homepage.
2. Let one memorable visual system carry the brand before explaining the product.
3. Preserve mystery while keeping the primary action unmistakable.
4. Show uncertainty as an intentional material, not a weakness.
5. Use restraint in UI chrome so the simulation field feels powerful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Reduced-motion users must receive a polished static composition. Do not rely on color alone for important meaning.
